Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

1. Overview

DSH rclone Drive Integration is a personal, non-commercial integration for accessing Google Drive through rclone and an AI-assisted local workflow.

2. Data Access

The application accesses Google Drive data only after the Google account owner grants OAuth authorization. Access is limited to the permissions granted by the user.

3. Data Usage

Google Drive data is used to perform operations requested by the account owner, including reading, uploading, organizing, and managing files.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

rclone stores OAuth credentials locally in its configuration file. Credentials are not published on this website.

When AI services are used to process Drive content, relevant data may be transmitted to those services according to their applicable privacy policies.

5. Data Security

The account owner is responsible for protecting local OAuth credentials and restricting access to the configuration files.

6. Revoking Access

The account owner may revoke Google Drive access at any time through their Google Account security settings.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support email listed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
